Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Your privacy is important to us. We’re committed to handling your personal information responsibly and protecting it throughout your insurance inquiry.

Information We Collect

When you complete our intake form, we may collect your name, contact information, location, household details, estimated income, insurance status, coverage preferences, and preferred coverage start date.

We may also collect referral information, including the name and phone number of the person who referred you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to better understand your insurance needs, explore potential coverage options, arrange phone consultations, and send appointment confirmations and reminders.

Referral information may be used to identify and verify referrals.

How We Protect and Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information or share it with unrelated third parties for marketing purposes.

Your information may be processed through Jotform and other service providers that support our intake forms, scheduling, and communications.

If additional information or authorization is needed to proceed with an insurance application or enrollment, we will discuss that with you separately.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Your Privacy Choices

You have the right to ask questions about your personal information, request corrections, or request deletion, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

You may also request that we discontinue further communications regarding your insurance inquiry.

Your preferences and requests will be respected in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how your information is handled, or your communication preferences, you may address them during your scheduled consultation or any follow-up communication regarding your inquiry.
